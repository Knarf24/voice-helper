🎙️ Multilingual Voice Helper

Many people can't easily type, read small print, or use English-only search. Here you ask out loud in your own language (isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Swahili, French and more) and hear the answer in that language: facts from Wikipedia, today's weather, sums, the time and date, or how to say a phrase in another language.

🐍 The helper's brain is Python running right here in your browser with Pyodide. Listening and speaking use your browser's built-in voice.

How it works

  1. Listen: your browser turns your speech into text in the language you picked (GeeksforGeeks Speech Recognition).
  2. Translate in: Python sends the words to the free MyMemory translator to get English (GeeksforGeeks Language Translator).
  3. Understand: Python works out what you want: a fact, a translation, the weather, a sum, or the time. Sums are worked out safely by Python itself.
  4. Look it up: facts come from Wikipedia (GeeksforGeeks Voice Search Wikipedia), weather from Open-Meteo.
  5. Translate out and speak: the answer is translated back to your language and read aloud (GeeksforGeeks Text to Speech).

Works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Firefox can't listen yet, but typing and reading aloud still work. Whether an answer can be read aloud in a language depends on the voices installed on your device; if there is none, the answer is shown as text.

🔒 Privacy: nothing is saved. To answer, your question is sent to the translator, Wikipedia or Open-Meteo, and in Chrome and Edge the browser's speech recognition sends your voice to the browser maker's servers.
